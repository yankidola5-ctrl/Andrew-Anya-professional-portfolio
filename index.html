FAQ

Before we start.

The questions clients ask most. If yours isn't here, just message me.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and the features you need, so every project is quoted individually. Message me a short description of your business and what you want, and I'll reply with a clear quote and timeline, usually within a day.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes about a week. A multi-page business site takes about two weeks, and larger sites or stores take three to four. The biggest factor is how quickly I get your logo, text and photos, so sending those early keeps things moving.

How does payment work?

I take a 50% deposit to start and the remaining 50% before I hand over the final files. I'll confirm the scope, price and delivery date with you in writing before any work begins.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included in every package. After you approve the design direction, changes are made to the built site. Larger changes of scope can be quoted separately.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (if you have one), a short description of your business, your contact details, and any photos you'd like to use. If you don't have text ready, I can help shape it. Tell me what you need and we'll work out the rest.

Who owns the website, domain and hosting?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and I help you set up hosting so the site is live and under your control. Hosting and domain costs are paid directly to those providers, separate from my fee.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site is built to work properly on phones, tablets and desktops, and I test it on all three before launch.

Can you add WhatsApp, forms or online ordering?

Yes. WhatsApp buttons, contact and booking forms, and simple ordering flows are common requests. Bigger features, like a full online store or a client portal, are scoped as part of a larger package.
